# 00 — Domain Glossary & Terminology 📖

## 1. Bilingual Terms (English / Tiếng Việt)

| English Term | Thuật ngữ Tiếng Việt | Context & Definition in VieBee |
|---|---|---|
| **Living Heritage** | Di sản sống | Intangible cultural knowledge, generational craft techniques, and living recipes practiced daily in local homes. |
| **Micro-Workshop** | Trải nghiệm vi mô | Short (90–120 minute), small-capacity (3–6 guests) skill-based cultural sessions hosted in family spaces. |
| **Cultural Host** | Người tổ chức / Đại sứ văn hóa | Everyday residents, home cooks, and elder craftspeople sharing their traditional skills. |
| **Independent Traveler** | Du khách tự túc | Tourists (primarily aged 20–45) seeking authentic immersion outside mass commercial tour buses. |
| **AI Cultural Copilot** | Trợ lý văn hóa AI | Bilateral translation and cultural context assistant powered by LangChain and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). |
| **Grounded Knowledge** | Dữ liệu kiểm chứng | Information strictly verified against the host's workshop sheet (ingredients, steps, etiquette, tools). |
| **GIS Cultural Map** | Bản đồ văn hóa số | Geographic discovery interface displaying nearby living heritage points of interest by proximity. |
| **Alleyway / Hẻm** | Hẻm phố Sài Gòn | Dense residential alleyways where authentic grassroots culture and family kitchens are nestled. |
| **QC Verification** | Kiểm soát chất lượng cộng đồng | On-site inspection of host identity, space safety, and technique authenticity before granting verified badges. |
| **Platform Take Rate** | Tỷ lệ hoa hồng nền tảng | The 10–15% (baseline 12%) fee retained by VieBee for platform operations, leaving 88% net to the host. |

## 2. Technical Abbreviations

- **P2P:** Peer-to-Peer marketplace.
- **RAG:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
- **GIS:** Geographic Information System.
- **RSVP:** Répondez s'il vous plaît (Booking reservation).
- **TAM:** Total Addressable Market (~21.15 Trillion VND).
- **SAM:** Serviceable Addressable Market (~2.86 Trillion VND).
- **SOM:** Serviceable Obtainable Market (50,000 paid bookings/year).
