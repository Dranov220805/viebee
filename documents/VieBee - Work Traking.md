**English:** VieBee \- Mapping Living Heritage, Empowering Communities

**Product Description:**  
**VieBee** is a peer-to-peer experiential travel platform connecting international travelers directly with grassroots local hosts. Powered by a GIS-based Cultural Map and real-time AI translation, it removes language barriers and enables ordinary locals to easily host and monetize authentic cultural workshops.

**CTA:** Discover Vietnam beyond the guidebooks through the people who live it.

**Idea Description**  
**1\. The Problem**  
Vietnam offers a rich range of food, crafts, traditions, and everyday cultural practices, but much of this knowledge remains difficult for travelers to discover and for local people to monetize directly.  
**Three problems exist simultaneously:**  
**1\. Travelers struggle to discover authentic micro-experiences.**  
Most tourism discovery is organized around destinations, attractions, and established tours. Travelers who want to learn a local recipe, make a traditional craft, or meet a neighborhood artisan have limited ways to find trustworthy experiences outside the mainstream tourism circuit.  
**2\. Local cultural knowledge is difficult to commercialize independently.**  
	Many artisans, home cooks, craftspeople, and community members possess valuable cultural knowledge but lack simple tools to promote their activities, manage reservations, communicate with international guests, and receive payments.  
**3\. Language and trust create friction.**  
	Travelers may hesitate to visit an unfamiliar local home or workshop, while hosts may feel uncomfortable communicating with foreign visitors. Both sides need clearer information, verification, scheduling, and communication support.

**2\. The Solution**  
VieBee connects travelers with verified local hosts through a cultural discovery marketplace built around workshops, stories, people, and places. A traveler can open VieBee, explore a Cultural Map, discover a nearby workshop, learn about the host and cultural story behind it, check available time slots, and RSVP.  
For the host, VieBee provides a simple way to:

1. Create a workshop  
2. Set availability and capacity  
3. Manage RSVPs  
4. Present their cultural story  
5. Communicate with travelers  
6. Receive payments  
7. Build trust through reviews and verification

**Core features**

- **Cultural Map:** A dynamic map showing workshops, artisans, community spaces, food traditions, and cultural activities.  
- **Micro-Workshop Marketplace:** Small-scale experiences such as traditional cooking, weaving, pottery, embroidery, bamboo crafting, local food preparation, and other community-led activities.  
- **Community Verification:** Host profiles and experiences are reviewed through identity verification, community validation, reviews, and platform moderation.  
- **AI Cultural Copilot:** AI-assisted translation and cultural context help travelers and hosts communicate while also explaining relevant cultural practices, etiquette, ingredients, techniques, and stories.

***A useful distinction:***  
LangChain \+ RAG is the technology behind the Cultural Copilot, not the product itself.

**4\. Target Users**  
VieBee is a two-sided marketplace with supporting ecosystem partners.  
**Primary demand side:**  
\-\> Independent international travelers  
Travelers who:

1. Are visiting Vietnam independently or in small groups  
2. Prefer authentic/local experiences  
3. Are comfortable using mobile apps or online booking  
4. Are interested in food, crafts, culture, and everyday life  
5. Prefer experiences that are shorter and easier to fit into an itinerary

**Primary supply side**  
\-\> Local cultural hosts and artisans  
Examples:

1. Home cooks  
2. Traditional craft makers  
3. Tailors  
4. Potters  
5. Weavers  
6. Farmers  
7. Family-run cultural spaces  
8. Elder artisans  
9. People with specialized local knowledge

**Secondary segments**  
\-\> Domestic experiential travelers

1. Young adults and families looking for meaningful weekend or short-trip cultural activities.  
2. Freelance tour guides  
3. Guides who can use VieBee's Cultural Map to add local workshops to personalized itineraries.  
4. Tourism partners  
5. Hotels, hostels, cultural organizations, and destination organizations that can refer travelers to VieBee.

**5\. Why Now / Market Opportunity**  
\-\> Vietnam's tourism market provides a substantial base for an experience marketplace.  
\-\> Vietnam received nearly 21.2 million international visitors in 2025, an increase of 20.4% year over year.  
\-\> In the first eight months of 2026, Vietnam recorded approximately 15.9 million international arrivals, up 14.4% from the same period in 2025\.  
\-\> Vietnam also recorded approximately 135.5 million domestic tourist trips in 2025, according to the Vietnam National Authority of Tourism.

This provides VieBee with a large underlying travel audience while allowing the company to begin with a much narrower, more manageable customer segment.  
The demand for cultural and community-based tourism is also supported by research in Vietnam. Recent studies identify authenticity, cultural engagement, community interaction, and perceived value as important aspects of community-based tourism experiences.

**6\. Competitive Landscape**

| Competitor | Main offering | Strength | Gap VieBee targets |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Airbnb Experiences | Local activities and experiences | Large global audience and established booking infrastructure | Broad marketplace; not specifically designed around community heritage preservation and hyper-local cultural mapping |
| Tubudd | Local buddies and customized local experiences | Strong local-person connection | Focuses primarily on personal local guides rather than structured, skill-based cultural workshops |
| Meetup | Local groups and events | Large community/event network | General-purpose events rather than tourism-focused cultural experiences |
| Luma | Event creation and registration | Simple event management and RSVP tools | General-purpose event platform rather than a Vietnam cultural marketplace |
| ***VieBee*** | ***Cultural workshops \+ Cultural Map \+ AI cultural support*** | ***Specialized community heritage model*** | ***Designed specifically around local cultural hosts and living heritage*** |

**Details:**  
Airbnb currently lists cooking, cultural tours, food tours, tastings and numerous hands-on workshops in Vietnam, showing that the category already has consumer demand. Airbnb also states that it typically charges 20% of the experience price as a host service fee, which gives VieBee a potential positioning around a simpler, community-friendly economic model, although the exact VieBee take rate should be validated.  
Tubudd explicitly positions its service around local buddies who accompany travelers and customize local itineraries.  
Meetup and Luma are general event/community platforms rather than platforms specifically designed for Vietnamese cultural heritage experiences.

**7\. VieBee's Competitive Differentiation**  
Three pillars to support:  
01 \- Living Heritage Marketplace  
Not simply "things to do," but experiences connected to people, skills, traditions, stories and places.

02 \- Cultural Map  
Instead of browsing only by activity category, travelers discover culture geographically:  
"What can I experience around me?"

03 \- AI Cultural Copilot  
AI reduces communication friction by providing:  
Translation → Context → Etiquette → Workshop information  
The AI should be grounded in verified VieBee knowledge and host-provided information rather than presenting itself as an unrestricted cultural authority.

**8\. Scalability & Growth Potential**  
The strongest scalability argument is not "AI makes everything cheap."  
Instead:  
VieBee is digitally scalable because the marketplace does not need to own physical tourism infrastructure. Hosts provide the experiences while VieBee provides discovery, trust, booking, communication and digital coordination.

The expansion model can be:  
Pilot neighborhood → City → Cultural destinations → Vietnam → Southeast Asia

**For example:**  
Phase 1: Ho Chi Minh City  
Phase 2: Hoi An \+ Hue \+ Hanoi  
Phase 3: Other cultural and craft destinations across Vietnam  
Phase 4: Selected Southeast Asian markets with similar community-tourism characteristics  
This is also consistent with the diversity of tourism products already being developed in Vietnam around heritage, cultural, community and sustainable tourism.

**9\. Social & Cultural Impact**  
VieBee should explicitly position impact as part of the business model rather than as a separate charity component.  
**1\. Economic impact**  
More tourism spending can reach individual hosts and small community businesses directly.  
**2\. Cultural impact**  
Traditional food, crafts, techniques and stories become economically valuable and therefore more sustainable.  
**3\. Social impact**  
Local residents become active participants in tourism rather than only consumers of tourism's economic effects.  
**4\. Tourism impact**  
Visitors are encouraged to explore beyond major attractions and interact with communities in smaller groups.

**10\. Revenue Model (Proposed)**  
**Primary revenue:**  
\-\> Transaction commission: VieBee takes a small percentage from each successfully booked experience.  
For example:  
Target platform take rate: 10-15%  
This should be presented as a proposed model to validate, not a finalized number.  
**Future revenue:**  
Potential future streams include:

1. Premium promotion for hosts  
2. B2B partnerships with hotels/hostels  
3. Tools for professional guides  
4. Destination/cultural organization partnerships  
5. Curated thematic collections or city passes
